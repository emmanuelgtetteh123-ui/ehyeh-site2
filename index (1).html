Our Story

The meaning behind Ehyëh

Ehyëh means “I am” — a reminder that we are always becoming. Every bag is crocheted by hand, one loop at a time, as a small practice in patience and care.

Because each piece is made by hand, no two are identical. The little differences aren't flaws — they're the signature of something made by a person, for a person.
